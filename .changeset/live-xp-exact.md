The Live screen's XP figures are now exact. XP per hour, the session XP total and a new measured XP per prop are read from the XP the game reports for every prop your heroes destroy, with every bonus included — the Pass, the Return Bonus, a Boost and hero runes — instead of being estimated from the props and reading up to half low. The measured XP per prop sits beside gold per prop and says how far it runs from the map's estimate. The download page's drawing of the Live screen shows the new figure.
