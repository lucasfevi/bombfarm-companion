Heroes' trips between targets now lengthen with both walk speed and fuse: a faster hero, or one with a longer fuse, walks farther to its next bomb. Walk speed and cooldown are therefore priced more accurately on the Farm board, the Optimizer and the Skill Tree, and gold/hr figures shift slightly.
