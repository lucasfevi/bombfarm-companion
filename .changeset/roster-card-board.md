See the whole roster at once, as a board of cards.

The Heroes screen has a view toggle above it. The rail stays what it always was — names down one
side, detail beside them — and the board is the other way of looking at the same roster: every
hero as a card, all of them on screen together.

**What a card shows.** The hero's identity and roll quality, then the three things you compare a
roster on and cannot see from a rail: its birth roll as eight tinted bars, one per statistic,
showing where each landed inside its own band; its ability pool, each icon with the level it sits
at out of twenty; and its gear, each piece with its item level and forge upgrade. The exact
reading behind every bar is on the bar's own tooltip, so the compact form loses nothing.

**Picking a hero from the board opens it.** The board fills the screen, so the detail is not
beside it — clicking a card selects that hero and returns you to the rail, where the detail is.
Picking from the rail does what it always did and leaves you where you are.

The switch is animated, and the cards arrive in order rather than all at once. A reader who has
asked their system for reduced motion gets neither.
