Sign in from Settings to see your Premium status. Press Sign in, finish in your browser, and come back signed in, with no password typed into the app. A Premium tab then shows whether Premium is active, why and until when, and warns you about a payment problem. Manage Premium opens your Premium page already signed in, and Sign out in Settings forgets the sign-in on this PC. The app also tells Premium which game account you play, so your purchase and your game account stay matched.
