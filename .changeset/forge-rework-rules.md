The Forge tab, the forge queue and the expected-cost figures follow the game's reworked Forge. Only
+1 to +4 always land now; a missed roll drops the piece one level, or to +10 from +12 up, and each
miss in a row adds 5 points to the next roll. The old safe jump to +8 is gone, so every rung is
rolled one at a time, and the expected gold now comes with the expected essence. From +12 up the
plan also prices the climb with the Protection Scroll, and notes that Chance Stones apply to a single
forge only.
