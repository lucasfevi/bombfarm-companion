One refresh bar under the top bar, the same on every tab.

**The refresh control has one home now.** A slim band under the top bar names the tab on
screen at its left and carries the read age and the Refresh button at its right — on Live, Farm,
Heroes, Inventory, Forge, Optimizer, PVP, Skill Tree and Account alike. Settings, which reads
nothing from the game, has no band. The four controls that used to sit in four different places
— over the Farm board's heading, over the Forge bag, over the Optimizer's title and inside the PVP
Standing panel — are gone from those screens.

**It knows what each tab is fresh from.** Farm, Forge and Optimizer compute from a copy they
take on open, so their line says "out of date" once the live account has moved past it and a
press recomputes over the account in hand and then asks for a new read, as before. PVP's line
dates the standing's own read ("as of 3m ago") and a press asks for the standing and the ranking.
Every other tab follows the live account, so the line is the age of the oldest section of the last
account read and a press asks main to read the account again. A press that starts no read — the
game closed, a press inside the 10 s floor, a fixture with no server — says why in the line's
place, wherever you are.

The Forge queue shares the band while a queue exists, on its own row beneath the refresh line.
