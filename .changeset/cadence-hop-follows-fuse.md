A hero near the cooldown cap now plants as fast as it really does. Measured on one hero respecced to the 80% cap on the compact map, its bomb goes off before the other heroes' blasts have cleared its neighbours, so its next target stands beside it: it hopped 0.57 of its usual distance in every density band and spent less time walking around live blasts, and its plant cycle halved where the model said it would shorten by a seventh. The Farm board and the Optimizer now shrink a hero's hop and its walking overhead as its fuse falls below 1.3 s, down to that measured share at the 0.40 s floor; a hero with a longer fuse is priced exactly as before. Cooldown reduction past about 35% is worth more plants than it was, and the Optimizer's point split can move toward it on heroes that walk fast.
