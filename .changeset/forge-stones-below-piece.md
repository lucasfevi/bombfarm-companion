The Forge screen's Chance Stone ranges now reach the levels a miss can drop a piece back to. A piece at +13 could only be given stones for +14 and +15; if the +14 roll failed and it fell to +12, the +13 roll ran with no stone and there was no way to set one. The ranges now start at +11 for any piece worn at +10 or above, so those re-rolls use the stone you choose.
