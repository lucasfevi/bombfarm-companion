The seven hero skins added by the 2026-09-26 game patch are supported: Mage, Dolphin, Vampire Count, Cauldron Witch, Shadow Ninja, Golden Paladin and Nurse. A hero wearing one now imports with its own avatar instead of an "Unknown skin" issue and the placeholder face, and an account that owns one prices it at its Steam market listing (`<Name> Skin`, e.g. `Nurse Skin`) alongside the five bought skins already known.
