PVP: the Optimizer and the Skill Tree now price a hero's cooldown on how fast heroes actually plant in duels, so fast-fuse heroes are valued higher. Heroes plant faster in a duel than on the farm, and the gap grows as the fuse shortens: about 1.55× the farm rate at the 0.4 s fuse floor, 1.27× at 1 s and 1.12× at 1.8 s. The duel objective now counts on that, so its point split can move toward cooldown. Gold and Gate clear are priced exactly as before.
