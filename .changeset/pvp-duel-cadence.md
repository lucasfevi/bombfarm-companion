PVP: the Optimizer and the Skill Tree now price a hero's cooldown on how fast heroes actually plant in duels, so fast-fuse heroes are valued higher. Heroes plant faster in a duel than on the farm, and the gap grows as the fuse shortens: about 1.49× the farm rate at the 0.4 s fuse floor, 1.25× at 1 s and 1.12× at 1.8 s. The duel objective now counts on that, so a hero already well into cooldown can be told to take it to the cap; a hero far from the cap keeps its points where they were. Gold and Gate clear are priced exactly as before.
