The Optimizer can gear heroes in a priority order. Turn on Priority order above the hero scope board, then drag the Optimize cards (or use their arrows) to rank them: hero 1 gets the best gear the pool offers, hero 2 the best of what is left, and so on, instead of the plan balancing gear across the whole team. Heroes lower in the order give up pieces to the ones above them, so the team total can end lower than today. The per-hero results can be sorted by priority.
