The Optimizer's switch is now "Ignore energy penalties", and turned on it does what it says. Before, a hero carrying Baton Pass could still be left ungeared or have gear taken off, because more energy means longer stints and so fewer pulses, and the plan counted that against the squad. With the switch on, the pulse and each hero's share of the bombing are held where today's gear puts them, so gear that adds energy never scores as a loss and every empty slot is filled when you own something that fits. Totals read higher than the roster really earns, as before.
