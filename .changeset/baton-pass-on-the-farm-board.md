Price Baton Pass on the Farm board and both Optimizer objectives as the team aura it is.

**A Baton Pass carrier was scored on the Farm board as if the ability did nothing, and the
Optimizer credited it to the carrier alone.** Baton Pass is a team aura that comes in pulses:
when a carrier enters the rotation, the whole field — heroes without the ability included —
deals +4% damage per rank for 120 seconds. Only the Optimizer's damage objective priced it, and
only for the carrier's own hits; the Farm board — and with it the Optimizer's gold objective and
the Farm respec advisor, which price through the same model — ignored it altogether.

**Every rotating surface now prices it the way the other team auras are priced.** Each carrier's
pulse lights the field for its own share of wall clock — 120 seconds of every rotation cycle its
stint and House rest add up to — overlapping pulses sum their ranks and clamp at the +80%
ceiling, and the clamp is taken inside the expectation, so two part-time carriers never read as
one permanent one. The Farm board goes one step further than a DPS figure can: it prices every
hero's hit through its own hits-to-kill step at each pulse level and averages the rates, so the
credit lands only where a pulse actually crosses a threshold. The Farm board, the Farm respec
advisor and both Optimizer objectives read the one rule; on the calibration anchor, one rank-20
carrier moved gold per hour by +1.0%, re-pinned and not refitted.

**A hero's own screen still leaves it out, and now says so.** The Heroes screen and the
planner's Combat tab price a hero's sheet, which the pulse never touches; their Effective-stats
tip names the two places that count it. The Optimizer's disclosures stop claiming the ability is
"modelled here only", and the ability's own description no longer reads "not modeled".
