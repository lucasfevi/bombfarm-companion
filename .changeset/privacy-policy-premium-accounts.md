The privacy policy now describes Premium accounts, in English and Portuguese: signing in with
Discord, Google or Steam keeps only each provider's account id (never an email, name or picture),
the game account link the desktop app can report, sessions stored only in scrambled form, where the
data is stored (a separate database in the eastern United States, with a nightly encrypted copy
kept for 90 days), how long it is kept, and how deleting the account works. Nothing changes for
anyone who never creates a Premium account. The page's date moves to 2026-10-13.
