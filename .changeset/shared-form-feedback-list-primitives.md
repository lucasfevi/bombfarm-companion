"@bombfarm/ui": minor

Add shared primitives for forms, feedback, lists and pagination: text input, text area and native select on one field chrome (with focus, placeholder and invalid recipe keys), a text link with a link-component slot, a skeleton, an error screen, a no-JavaScript pagination, definition and titled lists, a native disclosure, a status dot, a step progress, and a segmented toggle that submits as a form. Existing components gain options: a danger tone and medium size on the button, an info tone and flush layout on the banner, an inline size on the empty state, a danger tone and acknowledgement checkbox on the confirm dialog, a sort link on the data table header, and visible overflow on the panel.
