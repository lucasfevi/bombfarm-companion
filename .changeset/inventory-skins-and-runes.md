Show skins and runes in the inventory. An unpacked skin or a rune used to land under "Other" as a blank tile named only "Common". Each now has its own group. A skin is drawn with its avatar and named as the market lists it, for example "White Oracle". A rune is drawn with its sprite and named by the statistic it raises, with its tier below; hovering it shows what it raises and by how much, for example "Crit Damage +5%".
