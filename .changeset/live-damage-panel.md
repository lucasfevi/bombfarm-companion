The Live tab gains a Damage panel: Team DPS over the last 10 minutes and over the session, and for each hero its DPS, props destroyed and gold. Damage that cannot be tied to one hero is shown as Unattributed rather than guessed. Resetting the session restarts the damage figures along with the earnings, and the download page's Live preview shows the new panel. Earnings and damage also no longer stop counting after consent is granted again mid-session.
