The Combat tab on the web planner and the Combat stage on the desktop Heroes screen no longer end with a separate "Abilities & auras" panel. Its team-aura switches now sit inside the Effective stats panel, under their own "Team auras" heading, between the wired figures and the sheet-stats matrix they change — the same cards, the same switches and the same "+x% if on" / "−x% if off" promises. The own-abilities list is gone: the abilities a hero carries are already badged on the cards they reach, and listed on the hero's own Abilities panel.
